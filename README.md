# HR Employee Attrition Analysis Using MySQL

## Project Overview

This project analyses employee attrition data using **MySQL** to identify workforce groups associated with higher employee turnover.

The analysis was completed from the perspective of a data analyst supporting an HR department. The goal was to measure the organisation's overall attrition rate, compare attrition across employee groups, and provide practical recommendations that could support employee retention.

The dataset contains **4,653 employee records** and **9 fields** covering education, joining year, city, payment tier, age, gender category, bench status, current-domain experience, and employee attrition status.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, disrupt teams, and result in the loss of organisational knowledge.

The HR department wants to understand:

> **Which employee characteristics are associated with higher attrition, and which workforce groups should be prioritised for further investigation?**

---

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this analysis were to:

- Calculate the organisation's overall attrition rate
- Compare attrition across cities
- Analyse attrition by payment tier
- Determine whether bench status is associated with attrition
- Compare attrition across employee demographic categories
- Examine joining-year and experience patterns
- Identify employee groups that may require additional HR support
- Provide data-informed recommendations
